Test 5: Horizontal scrolling, mandatory snapping, pages of two cards

This is a typical carousel. The scroll container holds 10 cards in a single row. Each card is half the container width, so exactly two cards are visible at a time. Scroll snapping is mandatory (scroll-snap-type: x mandatory), but only the odd-numbered cards (1, 3, 5, 7, 9) are snap points. The container therefore always rests on a “page” of two cards: 1 and 2, 3 and 4, and so on. The scroll container has no role, name, or tabindex, like most scroll containers on the web. There are no scroll buttons on this page.
