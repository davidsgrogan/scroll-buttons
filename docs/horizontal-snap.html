Test 4: Horizontal scrolling, mandatory snapping on every card

The scroll container holds 10 cards in a single row. Each card is half the container width, so exactly two cards are visible at a time. Scroll snapping is mandatory (scroll-snap-type: x mandatory) and every card is a snap point (scroll-snap-align: start). The scroll container has no role, name, or tabindex, like most scroll containers on the web. There are no scroll buttons on this page.
