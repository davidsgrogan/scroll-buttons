Test 2: Vertical scrolling, mandatory snapping, one card per page

The scroll container is about 20rem tall and holds 8 cards stacked vertically. Each card is exactly as tall as the visible area, so one card is visible at a time. Scroll snapping is mandatory (scroll-snap-type: y mandatory) and every card is a snap point (scroll-snap-align: start). The scroll container has no role, name, or tabindex, like most scroll containers on the web. There are no scroll buttons on this page.
