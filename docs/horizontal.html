Test 3: Horizontal scrolling, no snapping

The scroll container is as wide as the page content (at most 40rem) and holds 10 cards in a single row. Each card is 40% of the container width, so about two and a half cards are visible at a time. There is no scroll snapping. The scroll container has no role, name, or tabindex, like most scroll containers on the web. There are no scroll buttons on this page.

    What to try

    What to check

    • Does the visible area scroll sideways as the reading cursor moves into a card that is hidden or partly hidden to the right?
